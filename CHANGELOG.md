# Changes

## 0.2 — Escort and ascension

- Added Astral Skiff arrival at 500,000 run score, clockwise companion orbit, blasts, charged laser and missiles.
- Added Solar Sovereign, Polar Sentinel, Quiet Oblivion and Afterlight transformations at 5M score and subsequent 10M-score intervals.
- Added 24 stellar skills across eight paths and run-scoped stellar points.
- Added 20 permanent ship modules with three ranks each, material costs, prerequisites and unlock feedback in Techno-fusion.
- Added available material balances and lifetime collection counters to archive cards.
- Migrated save format to version 2 without discarding existing discoveries or personal bests.
- Expanded and enlarged bosses with three new attack and movement patterns.
- Increased XP requirements progressively beyond level 100.
- Versioned the daily rules and standardized daily ship loadouts.
- Added deterministic regression coverage for economy, migration, milestones, skills and encounters.
